# THE KATANANI — approved artwork

These PNG files are unchanged copies from the user's KATANANI-Brand-Kit. Provenance records their SHA-256 hashes. The full wordmark is supplied artwork; it is not a font and must not be recreated by typing.

Core colors: petroleum #0A4D68, sky #D2E5F0, navy #2B3451, white #FFFFFF. The later identity conversation added lilac #B9AED8 and sage #A7C8B4 as secondary accents.

Keep original proportions and clear space of at least one quarter of the emblem height. Use the primary transparent artwork on white/sky. The user explicitly requested a white logo directly on dark/petroleum canvases: the renderer presents the original artwork alpha in white with an SVG color matrix, without a white header strip. The source PNG files remain unchanged.

Source Serif 4 and Noto Naskh Arabic are supporting title fonts for the bilingual campaign. They do not replace or reinterpret the supplied wordmark. Their licenses are in public/fonts.
